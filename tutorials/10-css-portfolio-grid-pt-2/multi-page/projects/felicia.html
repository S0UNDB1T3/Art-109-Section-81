Project 03 / Character Art

Felicia - Darkstalkers

This Felicia piece combines illustration, character art, and graphic design. I used multiple drawings of her in one composition so it feels loud, colorful, and full of movement.

Process

I approached the layout like an arcade poster or a page from a fighting game art book, then balanced the character poses with bold shapes and colors from the Darkstalkers series.
