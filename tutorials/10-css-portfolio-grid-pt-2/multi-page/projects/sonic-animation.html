Project 02 / 3D Animation

Sonic Animation Test

This Sonic piece was a render and animation test where I focused on posing, materials, and getting his personality to come through. I used an original image from the series as a reference while translating the look into 3D.

Process

I built the scene in Blender and worked on the timing, camera, lighting, and character pose so the final animation still feels energetic and faithful to Sonic's 2D design.
