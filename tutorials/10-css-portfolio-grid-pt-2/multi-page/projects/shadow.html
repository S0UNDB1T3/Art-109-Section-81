Project 01 / Illustration

I Am All of Me

This is a Shadow the Hedgehog piece I made around the moment where he is finally freed. I wanted the red lighting and heavy shadows to make the whole image feel dramatic, intense, and connected to his character.

Process

I created the illustration in Clip Studio Paint, then used After Effects to strengthen the atmosphere, lighting, and graphic elements.
